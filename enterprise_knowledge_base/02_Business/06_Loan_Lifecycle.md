# Northstar Lending Corporation
# End-to-End Loan Lifecycle

---

# 1. Purpose

This document describes the complete loan lifecycle at Northstar Lending Corporation.

It defines:

- Business processes.
- Customer interactions.
- Technology systems involved.
- Operational teams.
- Decision points.
- Key metrics.
- AI transformation opportunities.

The loan lifecycle begins when a potential borrower expresses interest and continues through loan closure.

---

# 2. Loan Lifecycle Overview

The Northstar lending lifecycle consists of ten major stages:
Customer Acquisition

 ↓

Application Submission

 ↓

Identity Verification

 ↓

Credit Assessment

 ↓

Loan Decisioning

 ↓

Loan Origination

 ↓

Funding

 ↓

Loan Servicing

 ↓

Collections

 ↓
Loan Closure

---

# 3. Lifecycle Stage Summary

| Stage | Primary Objective | Business Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Acquisition | Generate qualified borrowers | Marketing |
| Application | Capture borrower information | Digital Lending |
| Verification | Validate identity | Fraud/Risk |
| Credit Assessment | Evaluate creditworthiness | Credit Risk |
| Decisioning | Approve or decline loans | Underwriting |
| Origination | Complete loan setup | Lending Operations |
| Funding | Release funds | Payments |
| Servicing | Manage active loans | Loan Operations |
| Collections | Recover overdue amounts | Collections |
| Closure | Complete loan lifecycle | Servicing |
