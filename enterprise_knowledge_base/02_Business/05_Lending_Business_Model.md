# Northstar Lending Corporation
# Lending Business Model

---

# 1. Purpose

This document describes Northstar Lending Corporation's lending business model, core business capabilities, operating processes, stakeholders, and value creation model.

The document provides business context for technology modernization and AI transformation initiatives.

---

# 2. Business Model Overview

Northstar Lending Corporation provides digital lending products to consumers and small businesses.

The company creates value by:

- Providing access to credit.
- Evaluating borrower risk.
- Delivering fast lending decisions.
- Managing loan servicing activities.
- Supporting customers throughout the loan lifecycle.
- Managing credit risk responsibly.

The lending business balances three objectives:
Customer Growth

    +

Risk Management

    +

Operational Efficiency
