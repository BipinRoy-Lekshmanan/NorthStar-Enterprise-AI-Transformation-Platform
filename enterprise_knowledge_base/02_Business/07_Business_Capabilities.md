# Northstar Lending Corporation
# Enterprise Business Capability Model

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines Northstar Lending Corporation's enterprise business capability model.

A business capability represents what the organization must be able to do to execute its business strategy.

Capabilities are independent of:

- Organizational structure.
- Technology implementation.
- Specific applications.
- Current processes.

The capability model provides a foundation for:

- Enterprise architecture.
- Technology modernization.
- AI transformation.
- Investment prioritization.
- Business and technology alignment.

---

Northstar's business capabilities are organized into six major capability groups:

# 11. Capability Maturity Assessment

| Level 2 | Repeatable processes |

| Level 4 | Automated and data-driven |
