# 1. Purpose

This document provides an overview of Northstar Lending Corporation (NLC), including its business model, lending capabilities, organizational structure, technology landscape, and strategic transformation objectives.

This document establishes the foundational business context for technology strategy, architecture decisions, engineering practices, and enterprise AI transformation initiatives.

---

# 2. Executive Summary

Northstar Lending Corporation (NLC) is a technology-driven lending organization providing consumer and small business lending products through digital channels.

Founded in 1998, Northstar has evolved from a traditional lending institution into a digital financial services organization serving millions of borrowers across North America.

The company manages the complete lending lifecycle:

- Customer acquisition
- Loan application processing
- Identity verification
- Credit assessment
- Loan decisioning
- Origination
- Funding
- Account servicing
- Payments
- Collections
- Loan closure

Over the last decade, Northstar has expanded rapidly through digital investments and acquisitions. This growth has created opportunities for modernization, including:

- Simplifying legacy application landscapes.
- Improving engineering productivity.
- Increasing automation.
- Enhancing customer experience.
- Applying artificial intelligence throughout the lending lifecycle.

In 2026, Northstar launched a multi-year enterprise transformation initiative focused on becoming an AI-enabled, cloud-native lending platform.

The transformation vision:

> Build the most intelligent, scalable, and trusted lending technology platform by combining modern engineering practices, cloud capabilities, data intelligence, and responsible AI.
