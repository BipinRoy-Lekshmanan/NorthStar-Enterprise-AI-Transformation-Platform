# Northstar Lending Corporation
# Technology Operating Model

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the operating model used by Northstar Lending Corporation to plan, build, deliver, and operate technology capabilities.

The operating model establishes:

- How technology teams are organized.
- How business and technology collaborate.
- How investment decisions are made.
- How products are delivered.
- How engineering standards are governed.
- How AI transformation initiatives are executed.

---

# 2. Operating Model Vision

Northstar operates using a product-oriented, engineering-led operating model.

The objective is to move from a project delivery organization toward a continuous product delivery organization.

The target operating model enables:

- Faster customer value delivery.
- Stronger business and technology alignment.
- Clear ownership of business capabilities.
- Improved engineering productivity.
- Scalable AI adoption.

---

---

- Better domain knowledge.
- Faster decision-making.
- Reduced handoffs.

---

# 3.2 Business and Technology Partnership

Business and technology jointly own outcomes.

Technology is not treated as a delivery function only.

Teams are accountable for:

- Customer outcomes.
- Business value.
- Operational performance.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# 3.3 Platform Enablement

Northstar establishes shared platforms to enable product teams.

Examples:

- Cloud platform.
- Developer platform.
- Data platform.
- AI platform.
- Integration platform.

Product teams consume platforms rather than building duplicate capabilities.

---

# 3.4 Automation First

Automation is applied across:

- Software delivery.
- Testing.
- Infrastructure.
- Operations.
- Compliance.
- Documentation.

---

# 3.5 Responsible AI Adoption

AI adoption follows:

- Governance.
- Security review.
- Data privacy standards.
- Human oversight.
- Measurable business value.
